## 第二条　授权范围 / Article 2. Scope of Licence

2.1 甲方授予乙方以下**非独占**许可（如需独占，另行约定）：
- (a) 在乙方的“启扉 / Threshold”平台及其游戏中播放录音，包括网页、手机应用、微信小游戏；
- (b) 为播放需要进行格式转换、剪辑、降噪、调整响度、加入环境声或法器声；
- (c) 在乙方产品的介绍、宣传视频中使用不超过 [30] 秒的片段。

2.2 地域：[全球]。期限：自签署之日起 [五] 年，期满前 [三] 个月任何一方未书面提出终止的，自动续期 [五] 年。  
2.3 商业范围：乙方产品可以有广告和内购，但**录音不得出现在任何广告之中或与广告同时播放**，也不得用于售卖“功德”“福报”类商品。

2.1 Party A grants Party B a **non-exclusive** licence to:
- (a) play the Recordings within Party B's Threshold platform and its games (web, mobile apps, WeChat mini games);
- (b) convert, edit, denoise, normalise loudness, and add ambient or ritual-instrument sounds as needed for playback;
- (c) use excerpts of up to [30] seconds in product introductions and promotional videos.

2.2 Territory: [worldwide]. Term: [five] years from signature, renewing automatically for [five] years unless either party gives written notice [three] months before expiry.  
2.3 Commercial scope: Party B's products may contain ads and in-app purchases, but **the Recordings must never appear in, or play together with, any advertisement**, nor be used to sell "merit" or "blessings".

## 第三条　禁止事项 / Article 3. Prohibited Uses

乙方不得：
- (a) 用录音训练、克隆或合成任何人工智能声音模型；
- (b) 改动诵读的文字、音高或速度，使其表达与原文不同的内容；
- (c) 把录音用于与原宗教传统相冲突的内容，或用于嘲讽、恐怖、色情内容；
- (d) 让用户误以为录音来自甲方以外的人，或误以为甲方为乙方产品作宗教背书。

Party B shall not:
- (a) use the Recordings to train, clone or synthesise any AI voice model;
- (b) alter the recited text, pitch or speed so that it conveys something other than the original;
- (c) use the Recordings in content that conflicts with the tradition, or in mocking, horror or sexual content;
- (d) imply that the Recordings come from anyone other than Party A, or that Party A endorses Party B's product on behalf of the tradition.

## 第六条　撤回 / Article 6. Withdrawal

6.1 如甲方因宗教、个人原因书面要求撤回，乙方应在收到通知后 [30] 日内从产品中下架录音，并不再新增使用。已发布的宣传视频可保留，但不得再次投放。  
6.2 撤回不退还已支付的报酬或供养。  
6.1 If Party A asks in writing to withdraw for religious or personal reasons, Party B will remove the Recordings from its products within [30] days and stop new uses. Published promotional videos may stay but will not be promoted again.  
6.2 Withdrawal does not require refunding any fee or offering already paid.

## 第七条　保证 / Article 7. Warranties

甲方保证录音是本人诵读，有权签署本协议；乙方保证按本协议范围使用，并妥善保管原始文件。  
Party A warrants that the Recordings are their own performance and that they may sign this agreement. Party B warrants that it will use the Recordings only within this scope and keep the source files safe.

## 第八条　个人信息 / Article 8. Personal Data

乙方只为履行本协议保存甲方的身份和联系信息，不公开、不转让。署名按第四条执行。  
Party B keeps Party A's identity and contact details only to perform this agreement, and will not publish or transfer them. Attribution follows Article 4.
